# Category audit: targeted fact checks

Checked 2026-10-02 against first-party sources. Scope: five clues in `game/pack.js`; no game edits made.

| Clue | Result | Evidence and caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `wf-100` — largest animal known ever to have lived → blue whale | Supported | NOAA Fisheries identifies blue whales as the largest animals ever to live on Earth. The clue already includes “known,” which appropriately limits the historical superlative. For precision, “largest by mass” would avoid confusing size with length. [NOAA species account](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/blue-whale/science), “About the Species.” |
| `ps-400` — warm sparkling wine loses more dissolved gas when poured | Supported with scope caveat | Liger-Belair et al., *On the Losses of Dissolved CO₂ during Champagne Serving* (2010), experimentally compared the same Champagne at 4, 12 and 18 °C and two pouring methods. Higher temperature produced greater dissolved CO₂ loss during pouring. This directly supports the intended answer; the paper studied Champagne, so “generally” is a reasonable qualified extension to sparkling wine. Pouring method also changes the loss; compare the wines using the same method. [Original paper, DOI](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/jf101239w); [ACS-hosted full paper](https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/avweb/1c2web3536/champagnepouring.pdf). |
| `no-500` — Norwegian carpenter patented cheese slicer in 1925 → Thor Bjørklund | Correct person; date wording needs precision | Patentstyret confirms Bjørklund was a cabinetmaker and records the patent grant in **1926**, with priority date **27 February 1925**. Its article also uses the familiar shorthand “patented in 1925,” but the explicit grant/priority distinction is stronger evidence. Suggested clue: “This Norwegian cabinetmaker invented the cheese slicer in 1925. Who was he?” Patentstyret separately confirms the invention year. [Patent history](https://www.patentstyret.no/kundehistorier/ostehovel-i-100-ar); [official inventions overview](https://www.patentstyret.no/artikler/norske-oppfinnere-og-oppfinnelser). |
| `rt-500` — spirits ban formally repealed in 1927, referendum in 1926 | Supported | FHI’s official chronology explicitly distinguishes the 1926 referendum from the new alcohol law formally ending the spirits ban in 1927. It also dates repeal of the fortified-wine ban to 1923, so “spirits” is essential to the clue. [FHI chronology](https://www.fhi.no/le/alkohol/alkoholinorge/alkohol-i-historien/historisk-oversikt-alkohol-i-norge-1816-2019/), entries for 1923, 1926 and 1927. |
| `fd-100` — English Sesame Street’s Bert and Ernie; Norwegian says Sesam stasjon | English supported; Norwegian localization should be clarified | Sesame Workshop’s official Bert biography directly identifies Ernie as his best friend. NRK’s archive verifies that the local Sesam stasjon stories feature Alfa, Bjarne and Max (and Py in another episode). Those pages **do not establish that Bert/Ernie were absent from imported segments**. A primary source confirming their Norwegian dubbed names was not located; `nrk.no` and `tv.nrk.no` were inaccessible to the research tool. Safest evidenced change: retain the title **Sesame Street** in the Norwegian clue when using **Bert/Ernie**, e.g. “Bestevennene fra Sesame Street: Bert og …?” Do not assert that the pair never appeared on Sesam stasjon. [Official Bert biography](https://sesameworkshop.org/our-work/shows/sesame-street/sesame-street-characters/bert-2/); [NRK local-cast episode](https://arkiv.nrk.no/programoversikt/avansert/index603b.html); [NRK episode including Py](https://arkiv.nrk.no/programoversikt/avansert/index0dbc.html). |
