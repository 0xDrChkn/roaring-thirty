# Finish the Lyric: rebuilt five-clue set

Updated 2 October 2026 following the decision to keep this category. The executable set remains `finish-the-lyric`, with stable `fl-100`–`fl-500` IDs. This supersedes the old D-grade wording in `CATEGORY-AUDIT.md`; it does not settle tournament boards.

**Editorial grade: B, pending a guest rehearsal.** Familiar pop, rock and soul-pop songs cover the 1970s, 1990s, 2000s and 2010s. Difficulty now comes from recalling the continuation rather than supplying an announced song title. No missing answer is a song title. The upper three clues omit the artist and title. Recognition and difficulty remain assumptions about guests around age 30, not measured results.

| Tile | Verified song | Required next words | Intended difficulty and reason |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| `fl-100` | ABBA, Dancing Queen | 3 | Easy entrance: named performer, familiar refrain. |
| `fl-200` | Britney Spears, …Baby One More Time | 2 | Easy entrance: named performer, precise chorus cut replaces the old spliced fragments. |
| `fl-300` | Lady Gaga, Bad Romance | 3 | Medium: recall the continuation without an artist hint. The cut includes the pronoun that distinguishes this occurrence from a later chorus variation. |
| `fl-400` | Oasis, Wonderwall | 4 | Harder: recover a verse/pre-chorus phrase rather than its title refrain. |
| `fl-500` | Adele, Rolling in the Deep | 4 | Harder: exact continuation of a short cue, without the title or artist. Familiarity alone may not supply the whole phrase. |

The host awards points for all requested words in order. Spoken and sung answers are equally valid. Ignore accent, punctuation and capitalisation; the colloquial pronunciation/spelling of `fl-200` is explicitly accepted. Extra correctly continued words are fine. A song title alone, one remembered keyword or a paraphrase does not complete the task. The English lyric is identical in both interface languages, with translated instructions. Audio playback is not part of this category.

Rehearse the two high tiles with a non-player before settling their values. If the four-word continuations are immediate for the whole sample, replace those individual cuts; increasing obscurity is not automatically a fairer challenge. The existing First Letter token will reveal the first missing word’s initial, so include that token in the rehearsal.
