## Calibration and host use

The 100 tile is deliberately easy. E.T. and Back to the Future will still be quick for some guests; the goal is a relative party difficulty ramp, not obscure films. Groundhog Day and The Truman Show reward remembered stories. These values are editorial estimates, not results from guest playtesting. Rehearse with a few people and rebalance if both upper clues are immediate guesses.

Accepted English/Norwegian titles and bilingual explanations appear after answer reveal. No release year is required. The Back to the Future clue specifically targets the first film, while the Lion King clue openly accepts either version. The host should not reveal explanation text as an ad hoc hint before scoring.

Render all five clues on the actual party TV before the event. No complex person/family emoji sequences are used, but the clock, snow cloud, bed and brick still need a readability check from the back of the room. Code checks cannot establish TV readability or guest difficulty.
